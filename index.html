Timing

The twins were born in August 1947, so they were conceived around November–December 1946 (twins often arrive a few weeks early). That's just after the war: many men were still in uniform or waiting to be demobbed, and young men were still being called up. RAF Stanbridge was on Leighton Buzzard's doorstep, and RAF Cardington and RAF Chicksands were elsewhere in Bedfordshire. He could easily have been a serviceman who went home to Shetland, or to wherever his family had moved, afterwards.
